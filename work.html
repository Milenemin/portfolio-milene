Tango Urquiza

The Work

Redesigned the full Tango Urquiza website, including UX structure, visual identity, interactive prototyping, and front-end development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Supabase.

The Challenge

Tango Urquiza is a tango school in the heart of Copenhagen. The goal was to update their website to improve user-friendliness, reduce visual clutter, and create a more playful and engaging visual identity.

The Solution

Through user research, including interviews and a 5-scale questionnaire, we identified key user needs and design preferences. I contributed by conducting research, developing prototypes in Figma, and translating the final design into code. The process included iterative testing and refinement to ensure a cohesive visual identity and improved usability across devices.

Color Palette

The color palette aligned with the brand identity of Tango Urquiza. It combines controversial and playful tones for tango , while remaining inviting and accessible. The contrast helps guide user attention and supports a clear, engaging navigation experience.

Weekly Schedule

The weekly schedule was redesigned to be more visually engaging and user-friendly. The classes are color-sorted by level, which during user testing was found to be an important aspect while signing up for a class. The color palette here differs slightly, for added dynamism.

Creative Escape

The Work

Design from scratch, user research, UX, front-end in HTML, CSS and implementation in Astro

The Challenge

Creative Escape is a digital platform for young creatives aged 16–27, designed to help them explore Berlin beyond the tourist trail. The goal was to build a site that felt functional and relevant to this specific user group.

The Solution

The website designed is modern with a clean yet playful identity — featuring hand-drawn, urban typography and a dynamic color palette. Based on user research, activities were organized into three categories: Upcoming events, Free events, and Not This... But That — catering to budget-conscious creatives looking for local, alternative experiences.

Color Palette

The color palette aligned with the brand identity of Creative Escape. It features bold tones and white / black for a synthesis of youthfulness and user-friendliness.

Graphic Element

A graphic element I designed for the “Free Events” category. It allows fast recognizability and an additional element to the hand-drawn and playfulness.

Blooming Beauty

The Work

Design of fictional company from scratch, UX, prototyping and front-end implementation in HTML, CSS and JS.

The Challenge

Blooming Beauty is a fictional skincare brand created to explore brand identity, user experience, and interactive design. The goal was to develop a visually cohesive and engaging universe that appealed to a younger audience while maintaining trust and inclusivity

The Solution

My role focused on concept and experience design. I developed prototypes, generated AI visuals aligned with the brand’s tone of voice, designed and coded an interactive “Discover Your Skin Type” quiz to guide users toward relevant products. To strengthen engagement, I created a timed pop-up inviting users to “Join our Blooming Community,” reinforcing an inclusive and welcoming brand feel.

Color Palette

The palette combines earthy hues, with a vibrant coral accent tone to communicate clarity, professionalism, and reliability within a B2B packaging context. Strategic use of color supports hierarchy and reinforces the logic of the Tòki Flow model.

Quiz

An interactive 'Find Your Skin Type' quiz I designed with soft colors, inviting buttons, and hover effects — guiding users to personalized product recommendations."

Toki

The work

Designed and developed the website from scratch, including branding and UX research. Front-end implementation in HTML, CSS and Supabase.

The Challenge

Toki was a newly established B2B packaging company without existing cases or a defined visual identity. The challenge was to create a strong, credible brand presence and communicate their five-phase “Toki Flow” model.

The Solution

I contributed by developing multiple interactive prototypes in Figma and refining the layout structure across key pages, including the Toki Flow model and case overview. Through iterative user testing, adjustments were made in regards to strengthening navigation clarity.

Color Palette

Calm, earthy hues are paired with playful accents to evoke a sense of tranquility and inclusivity. The palette enhances the brand’s approachable identity while supporting an engaging and visually cohesive user experience.

Weekly Schedule

A case display I developed using Supabase, an open-source BaaS platform, where four fictional cases of Tokis products are showcased.

Poetry by Milene

The Work

Designed and developed a conceptual poetry website (own poetry) combining work with photographers and audio, front-end implementation in HTML and CSS.

The Challenge

The goal was to create a digital space that could present my poetry and photography in a cohesive and atmospheric way — without overdesigning or distracting from the art itself. The challenge was balancing simplicity with emotional depth

The Solution

I designed and coded a minimal, 90s-inspired interface that allows each poem and image to stand independently. I collaborated with photographers to ensure visual coherence between text and imagery. Each section includes a voice note (MP3) of me reading a poem, adding intimacy and dimensionality to the experience. As a dedicated writer of poetry, this project highlights my strength in storytelling, tone, and conceptual cohesion — skills that strongly inform my design work.

Color Palette

A minimal, pastel palette was chosen to let typography and imagery take focus, creating a calm and atmospheric space recalling seasonal hues. Subtle contrasts enhance readability while preserving a soft, introspective tone.
